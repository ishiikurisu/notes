# Types of Headache

> WARNING!

I am not a doctor! These notes are based on personal observation and what has worked for me. This might not be true for everyone else.

## Tension Headache

![Tension headache](./web_graphics/headache/tension_headache.png)

- This is the headache when you feel tired.
- Stop for a while, take a nap, and drink water.

## Cluster Headache

![Cluster headache](./web_graphics/headache/cluster.png)

- This headache is characterized by **a lot** of pain around one eye. They are popular known as the "suicide headache".
- Treatment should be done with a doctor.

## Migraine

![Migraine](./web_graphics/headache/migraine.png)

- Migraines are a set of different headaches, usually associated with nausea and sensitivity to other stimuli. It is often preceded by an aura.
- It can be caused by a variety of factors such as hormonal changes; environmental changes; and not sleeping well.
- Ibuprofen or paracetamol can be used for management.

## Sinusitis

![Sinusitis](./web_graphics/headache/sinus.png)

- Inflammation of the sinuses can cause headaches as well, usually felt as pain around the nose and between the eyes.
- This can be caused by allergies and viral infections.
- Treatment will depend on the cause but might include vaccinations; pain killers; and changes to the environment.
